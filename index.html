The science

Predict the food noise before it hits.

GLP-1 medications suppress appetite by maintaining a steady concentration in your bloodstream. As your dose wears off — typically day 5–7 — that suppression fades, and food noise returns.

Glide models this using first-order pharmacokinetics and alerts you before your concentration crosses the critical 40% threshold.

  • Semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy) — 5-day half-life modelled
  • Tirzepatide (Mounjaro, Zepbound) — 5-day half-life modelled
  • Crossover point calculated to the hour
  • Live Activity updates in real time on your lock screen
