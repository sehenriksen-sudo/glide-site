1Data stored on your device

The following data is created and stored exclusively on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it via the CSV export feature.

Data typeWhat it contains

This data is protected by your device's standard security (Face ID / passcode encryption).

2Apple HealthKit

Glide can read workout data from Apple Health with your explicit permission. We use this data solely to populate the exercise log inside the app.

Apple requirement: HealthKit data is never used for advertising, never sold to third parties, and never used for any purpose other than providing exercise tracking features within Glide.

You can revoke HealthKit access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Glide.

3Community chat (optional, anonymous)

The community chat feature is opt-in and anonymous. If you use it:

  • Firebase Anonymous Authentication — assigns you a randomly generated anonymous ID with no connection to your name, email, Apple ID, or any other identifier.
  • Firebase Firestore — stores your community profile (display name, bio, GLP-1 type, weeks on journey, optional photo) visible to other users while you are active.
  • Firebase Storage — stores your profile photo, linked only to your anonymous ID.
  • Chat messages — stored for the duration of an active session only. When either participant disconnects, the session is marked inactive and messages are not retained.

No email address, phone number, Apple ID, real name, location, or device identifier is associated with your community profile. See Google's Privacy Policy for Firebase infrastructure details.

4Subscriptions (RevenueCat)

Glide uses RevenueCat to manage Premium subscriptions. When you make a purchase, RevenueCat processes the transaction through Apple's App Store. RevenueCat may collect purchase history, subscription status, and a device-level anonymous identifier. They do not receive your name, email, or payment details — those remain with Apple.

See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy for details.

5Crash reporting & analytics

Glide does not include any third-party analytics SDK. We do not track in-app behaviour, screen views, or usage patterns.

Apple may collect anonymised crash reports through the App Store if you have opted in to "Share with App Developers" in your iPhone settings. We use these solely to fix bugs.

6Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties except:

  • Apple — to process App Store purchases and deliver the app.
  • Google Firebase — only for anonymous community chat sessions (section 3).
  • RevenueCat — only for subscription management (section 4).
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.

7Children's privacy

Glide is rated 12+ on the App Store. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the community features, contact us and we will remove their data promptly.

8Your rights

  • Delete your data — delete the app to erase all on-device data. To delete your anonymous Firebase community profile, use the in-app profile settings or contact us.
  • Export your data — use Settings → Export Data in the app to download a CSV of all your logs.
  • GDPR (European users) — you have the right to access, correct, port, and erase your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing.
  • CCPA (California users) — we do not sell personal information. California residents may request disclosure of data categories collected.
